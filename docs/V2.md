# V2 — brief from the first review with the client

Feedback (Salah, The Chefz): loves the floating letters and the opening moment.
Asks for:

1. **The latest letters are the ones flying** — ideally *all* letters flying,
   arranged beautifully.
2. **Sound** when a letter opens (generated sounds; sound design matters).
3. **More generated imagery** that feels premium ("legendary") — wax seals and an
   envelope hero were generated on Higgsfield (see `src/lib/icons.json`).
4. **UI that doesn't look AI-made.**
5. **The opened letter**: the coloured line at the top of the letter + the round
   heart badge look bad → rethink the letter's shape.
6. **Mobile first**: most visitors come from social links on phones — flexible,
   easy, excellent on mobile.
7. **Brand**: use the **Molhim** font across the whole site (`font-sans` now
   resolves to Molhim → IBM Plex Sans Arabic fallback for Latin/«»/—/…), and the
   **official The Chefz logo** (`public/brand/thechefz-logo.webp`, 497×120,
   two blocks: plum "THE" + orange "CHEFZ").

Notes on Molhim: Arabic + digits only (ASCII digits render as Arabic-Indic
glyphs — fine). No Latin letters, no «» — those fall back to Plex automatically.
Use `font-[family-name:var(--font-latin)]` (or the `--font-latin` variable) for
phone/email inputs and URLs. Aref Ruqaa (`font-hand`) is now ONLY for small
handwritten touches: signatures, the rubber stamp, the hover name label in the
3D scene. Everything else — headings, UI, letter bodies — is Molhim.

## Design direction — "real stationery, not a template"

Avoid the AI-template tells: accent bars on rounded cards, emoji as section
markers, everything centred, pills everywhere, identical shadows on every box,
gradient badges. Instead, borrow from **real correspondence**:

- **Paper**: warm off-white sheet `#fffdf8` with a subtle SVG noise grain
  (feTurbulence, ~4% opacity) and, for letters, faint **triangle-fold creases**
  (the letters travel folded as triangles in the 3D scene — when opened, the
  creases remain: two soft diagonal shadow lines meeting at the centre-top).
- **Envelope**: the writer's colour lives in the **envelope**, not in a stripe.
- **Wax seal**: the Higgsfield seal render (`sealFor(m)`), or an SVG seal
  fallback (irregular circle in the accent colour with an embossed heart/dove).
- **Postage stamp** (perforated edge via CSS mask/radial-gradient) holding the
  card colour's 3D icon (`cardStyle(m).icon`) + a round **postmark** over it
  ("يوم المعلم · ٥ أكتوبر") drawn in SVG with `textPath`.
- **Rubber stamp** «شكراً معلمي» / «في ذكراك 🤍» — keep, refine (ink texture via
  mask, double border, slight rotation).
- Type: Molhim for everything; generous line-height for Arabic (1.9–2.1 for
  body); real hierarchy (size + weight + colour), fewer boxes.
- Layout: start-aligned (right) editorial layout for section headers instead of
  centring everything; asymmetric where it helps.
- Emojis only inside the campaign copy sentences from the brief, never as
  decoration on headings/badges/buttons.

## Features

### A. The flying letters are the letters (scene + immersive mode) — owner: **scene**
- Hero field shows the newest letters (`heroLetters` = `getSceneLetters()`, up to
  40). Filler letters only fill up when there are fewer than ~24 real ones.
- **Immersive "all letters" mode** (the reference's "Endless Letter"): a clear
  CTA in the hero «تجوّل بين كل الرسائل» opens a full-screen overlay
  (`src/components/scene/ImmersiveLetters.tsx`, default export, props
  `{ open: boolean; onClose(): void; onOpen(id: string): void; seed: PublicMessage[] }`):
  - its own `LettersEngine` instance in an `immersive` mode: the camera travels
    forward through an endless tunnel of letters; wheel / trackpad / vertical
    touch-drag / ↑↓ keys move forward/back; slow auto-drift when idle;
    letters that pass behind the camera recycle to the far end carrying the
    NEXT letters from the list, so over time every letter flies by.
  - pages through `/api/messages?sort=new&limit=30&cursor=…` as the camera
    advances (dedupe by id; wraps around at the end).
  - hover label + click/tap opens the letter view (above the overlay, z-index);
    closing the letter returns to the tunnel.
  - chrome: a top bar with the logo (small), a counter «رسالة ١٢ من ٢٤٠», close ✕
    (Esc), a hint on first entry («مرّر أو اسحب لتتنقل بين الرسائل»), SoundToggle.
  - mobile: vertical swipe moves, tap opens; respects reduced motion (no
    auto-drift, step with buttons ↑↓ instead).
  - engine stays paused when the overlay is closed; dispose on unmount.
- `NEW_LETTER_EVENT` fly-in stays for the hero; also play `play("send")` is the
  form's job.

### B. The opened letter — owner: **letter**
Rebuild `LetterModal` around **an envelope and a sheet**:
- Backdrop: calm cream with a very soft tint of the letter colour.
- Mobile (default): full-screen. The **envelope** (card colour, subtle paper
  grain, the inner flap as a darker triangle) peeks at the top; the **sheet**
  slides up out of it and fills the screen width (16px gutters). Desktop: the
  envelope sits behind/below a centred sheet (max ~640px), slightly rotated.
- Sheet top: the **wax seal** (broken in two halves after opening — two SVG/
  image halves nudged apart) centred on the fold; the **postage stamp +
  postmark** at the top-start corner (start = right).
- Address block: «إلى» small, then the name (Molhim bold ~1.9rem mobile /
  2.4rem desktop), then school — like an address on stationery, NOT a card
  header. No coloured top line. No round heart badge.
- Body: Molhim ~1.2rem/2.05 mobile, 1.35rem desktop, `white-space: pre-line`,
  faint ruled lines aligned to the line-height (only behind the body).
- Closing: «— <fromName>» in `font-hand` accent colour + date; the rubber
  stamp slanted across the corner.
- **Opening animation** (the client loved it): a triangle-folded letter
  appears, its three flaps unfold in 3D (CSS `rotateX/rotateY`, perspective)
  into the full sheet (~650ms), the seal cracks, and `play("open")` fires on
  the user's click that opened it (call it from the opener; for deep links
  there is no gesture → no sound). Reduced motion → fade.
- Actions: **mobile sticky bottom bar** (safe-area aware): ❤ like (count) ·
  مشاركة (opens the share sheet) · 🎁 هدية (hidden for memory letters).
  Report as a quiet text button at the end of the sheet. Desktop: the same
  actions in a row under the sheet.
- Gestures: swipe down on the sheet header (≥ 100px) closes on mobile;
  horizontal swipe steps prev/next; desktop arrows + ←/→ keys.
- Memory letters: grey-lavender envelope, dove seal, «في ذكراك 🤍» stamp,
  «دعوة بالرحمة» like label, no gift, no playful motion.

### C. The wall — owner: **wall**
- Cards become **envelope fronts / folded notes** instead of rounded boxes with
  a top stripe: the card colour as the paper tint (keep the lively wall), a
  small postage stamp (colour + icon) in the top-end corner, the address
  block («إلى» + name in Molhim bold + school), 5–6 lines of the letter,
  signature in `font-hand`, a folded corner at the bottom-start, date as a tiny
  postmark. Actions row: like · share · «⋯» (report). Tap anywhere opens.
- Section header start-aligned: title + the marketing line on the start side,
  search right under it at full width on mobile.
- Like → `play("like")`.
- Search results share + invitation empty state stay.

### D. Shell — owner: **shell**
- Hero: remove the floating emoji/icon decorations (the scene is the visual).
  Headline in Molhim (keep «معلّم» emphasis in orange with the hand-drawn
  underline). Add the «تجوّل بين كل الرسائل» CTA (opens ImmersiveLetters) next
  to «اكتب رسالتك». The counter as a handwritten-style note, not a pill.
  Mobile hero: headline larger, fewer lines, CTAs full-width stacked.
- Pass `heroLetters` from `getSceneLetters()` in `page.tsx` and `m/[id]/page.tsx`.
- **Form as writing a letter**: the paper sheet reads like a letter being
  written: «إلى [أستاذ/أستاذة/د.▾] [الاسم____]» on one line, «في [المدرسة/
  الجامعة____]», the ruled message area, «من: [اسمك____]». Inputs styled as
  underlines on the paper (keep ≥44px hit areas and visible labels for a11y —
  labels can be the inline words). The colour picker becomes «لون الظرف»
  (envelope swatches — small envelope shapes, not dots). «في ذكرى» and the
  surprise opt-in live in a compact «خيارات» area under the sheet.
- On send: the sheet folds into a triangle and flies up (keep) + `play("send")`.
- Success panel: use `Icon3D name="envelope"` as the hero image.
- Header: official logo; mobile header = logo + «اكتب رسالتك» + gift icon.
- `SoundToggle` fixed at the bottom-end corner (like the reference), above the
  safe area; hidden while the preview banner covers it is not a concern.
- `preloadSounds()` on first pointerdown anywhere.

### E. Everywhere
- Mobile at 360/390px is the primary design target; desktop is the enhancement.
- No horizontal overflow; tap targets ≥ 44px; inputs ≥ 16px.
- Keep all contracts in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (API, provider, types).
